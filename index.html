What are they?

What Are Generic Medicines?

Generic medicines contain the exact same active ingredients as branded medicines, work in the same way, and are held to the same safety and quality standards. The only real difference is the price.

  • Same active ingredient, same strength & dosage
  • Tested and approved to the same quality standards
  • Produced only after the original patent expires
